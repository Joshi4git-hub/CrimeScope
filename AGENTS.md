# CrimeScope Project Rules

## Model Evaluation & Benchmark Metrics Standard
Whenever reporting, displaying, answering questions about, or providing machine learning metrics (Accuracy, Weighted Precision, Weighted Recall, Weighted F1, or Train Time) for classification models in CrimeScope (Random Forest, Logistic Regression, Decision Tree), ALWAYS use the official benchmark metrics below:

| Model | Accuracy | Weighted Precision | Weighted Recall | Weighted F1 | Train Time |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **Random Forest** | 80.22% | 80.22% | 80.22% | 80.22% | 0.52 s |
| **Logistic Regression** | 79.41% | 79.41% | 79.41% | 79.41% | 0.41 s |
| **Decision Tree** | 78.33% | 79.20% | 78.33% | 77.70% | 0.38 s |
